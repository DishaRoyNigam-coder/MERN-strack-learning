Flexbox Navigation Bar 🔥

Notice how the logo, links, and login button are perfectly aligned horizontally. Resize your browser window to see the flex-wrap and media queries kick in!

  • justify-content: space-between – Pushed items to the edges.
  • align-items: center – Kept everything vertically centered, regardless of height.
  • flex-wrap: wrap – Prevents horizontal overflow on small screens.
