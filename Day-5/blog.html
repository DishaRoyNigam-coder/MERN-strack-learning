Day 2: Learning Semantic HTML

Published on August 6, 2026 by Your Name

What are Semantic Tags?

Today I learned about semantic HTML tags. Unlike generic <div> tags, semantic tags like <article> and <nav> give meaning to the content. This is crucial for accessibility and SEO.

Breaking Down My Layout

I used <header> for my site banner, <nav> for the menu, <main> for the core content, and <article> for this blog post. Inside the article, I split the text into logical <section> elements.

Why Accessibility Matters

Screen readers rely on these tags. A visually impaired user can jump directly to the <main> content without listening to the navigation over and over.

What's Next?

Tomorrow, I'll dive into HTML forms and inputs. But for now, I'm proud of this clean, semantic structure.
